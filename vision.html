"Every queen carries not just a crown, but the dreams and aspirations of her community."

What We Stand For

Our Three Pillars

Beauty with Purpose

We celebrate beauty not as an end in itself, but as a vehicle for impact. Every contestant is encouraged to carry her platform with intention and grace, inspiring those around her.

Culture with Pride

Rooted in the heritage of Donkro Nkwanta and the Bono East Region, Miss Sankofa celebrates the richness of our traditions, language, and identity as a source of strength.

Youth with Vision

We believe in the transformative power of young women with clear goals. Miss Sankofa equips contestants with skills, confidence, and community to lead their generation forward.

Our Mission

Nurturing Changemakers, One Queen at a Time

To identify, train, and empower young women to become cultural ambassadors, community leaders, and change agents by blending Ghanaian traditions with modern leadership, advocacy, and social impact through beauty pageantry, education, and community development initiatives.

Our queens don't just wear a crown — they carry a responsibility. Each winner leads a community-focused project during her reign, turning her title into tangible impact for the people of Donkro Nkwanta and beyond.

Since 2022, every Miss Sankofa has championed the Voice for Children initiative — advocating for child safety and delivering essential resources to schools across the region.
