About Us

The Organization Behind Miss Sankofa

Miss Sankofa is the flagship cultural and empowerment organization behind Donkro Nkwanta's most celebrated annual event. Since 2016, it has grown from a local celebration into a platform for leadership, advocacy, and youth development across the Bono East Region.

Our mission is to identify, train, and empower young women to become cultural ambassadors, community leaders, and change agents — blending Ghanaian tradition with modern leadership, education, and social impact. Since 2022, every queen has championed Voice for Children, our advocacy initiative against child abuse.

Voice for Children

Voice for Children is Miss Sankofa's flagship advocacy initiative, launched in 2022 to raise awareness about and speak out against child abuse in Donkro Nkwanta and the wider Bono East Region. Since its launch, every reigning Miss Sankofa queen has served as an ambassador for the cause, carrying the message into schools, churches, and community gatherings throughout her reign.

The initiative focuses on education and prevention — helping children understand their right to safety, and helping parents, teachers, and community members recognize the signs of abuse and know how to respond. It reflects the pageant's broader belief that a queen's platform should extend well beyond the crowning night into real, sustained community impact.

  • Launched in 2022 as the pageant's core advocacy platform
  • Champions education and awareness against child abuse
  • Carried forward by every Miss Sankofa titleholder during her reign
  • Engages schools, families, and community groups across the region

Impact

Community Clean-Up Drive

As part of Miss Sankofa's ongoing commitment to civic responsibility, this year's contestants organized and led a community-wide clean-up exercise in Donkro Nkwanta. The initiative brought together residents, local volunteers, and pageant organizers in a shared effort to promote a cleaner, healthier environment for the town.

Beyond the physical clean-up, the event served as an opportunity for contestants to engage directly with community members, reinforcing the pageant's broader mission of leadership through service. The drive is part of a growing tradition where every Miss Sankofa cohort is expected to give back to the community that supports them.

  • Led by current Miss Sankofa contestants and volunteers
  • Focused on public spaces and market areas in Donkro Nkwanta
  • Part of the pageant's wider civic and community impact programming

Applications Now Open — Miss Sankofa 2027

Registration for the next edition of Miss Sankofa is officially open. Since 2016, the pageant has trained and empowered young women from the Bono East Region to become cultural ambassadors, community leaders, and advocates for social change — and the search for the next cohort is now underway.

Selected contestants join a structured programme that combines cultural education, leadership training, and public speaking development, culminating in the crowning ceremony held every 2nd of January. Every reigning queen also becomes an ambassador for Voice for Children, the pageant's advocacy initiative against child abuse.

  • Open to eligible young women from Donkro Nkwanta and the wider region
  • Includes leadership, cultural, and public speaking training
  • Crowning ceremony held annually every 2nd January
  • Winner becomes an ambassador for the Voice for Children initiative
